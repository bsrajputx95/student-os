# Local company artwork

Retrieved 21 October 2026. All 67 assets are served from `public/brands/` without third-party image requests from student pages. `shared/content/brands.json` records each source, format, collection and retrieval date. `node scripts/import-brand-assets.mjs` downloads missing assets and preserves existing files; use `--refresh` to request all sources again. A failed refresh retains a previously downloaded file.

56 SVGs come from [Simple Icons 11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0), whose collection is distributed under CC0. Brand marks remain subject to their owners’ trademark and usage rules; see the [Simple Icons disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md). The other 11 assets are icons declared by the companies’ official websites and retain their owners’ rights. No partnership or endorsement is implied. Unlisted employers use initials instead of invented logos.

The 40 new company directory entries in `shared/content/company-expansion.js` link to official careers sources reviewed on 21 October 2026. They contain original StudentOS preparation suggestions and role tags; they do not claim current vacancies, salaries, campus partnerships or past interview questions. Some external sites require JavaScript or block automated access. Intel uses the Workday destination linked by its [official application instructions](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/articles/000089205/programs/jobs-at-intel.html). Zomato links to [Eternal’s current careers page](https://www.eternal.com/careers/).
